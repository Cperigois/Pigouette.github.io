# About Me

I am **Carole Périgois**, a scientist driven by curiosity, creativity, and the ambition to bridge rigorous research with real-world impact. My background in **high-energy physics** and **astrophysics** has led me to work on some of the most fascinating questions in modern science: black holes, gravitational waves, and the statistical modeling of complex data. Beyond the lab, I am passionate about collaborative work, mentoring, and applying **data science and AI** to societal and environmental challenges.  

I thrive in **interdisciplinary environments** where innovation meets concrete applications, and I value strong teamwork, leadership, and open communication as the foundation for successful projects.
